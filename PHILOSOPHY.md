# Philosophy

*This is the foundation. The metaphor and the seven guidelines below are the constitution of the build. Every architecture and product decision in the other docs must trace back to here.*

---

## The one line

> There's no view from nowhere in a divorce. We don't find the truth — we build the smallest reality both people can live in.

---

## The diagnosis: there is no view from nowhere

Two people, two complete and internally-consistent accounts of the same marriage. The "slights" are real *in their frame*. Neither is lying. There is no fact-of-the-matter about whose reality is correct.

This is not loose metaphor. It has real structure:

- **Relational quantum mechanics (Rovelli):** a system's state is not absolute; it is only ever defined *relative to an observer*. There is no God's-eye account.
- **Wigner's friend / Frauchiger–Renner:** two observers can hold *contradictory* facts about the same event and both be internally consistent. No experiment adjudicates between them.
- Philosophy got there first. Nagel's "view from nowhere" doesn't exist; Kurosawa's *Rashomon* is the whole thing in 88 minutes; late Wittgenstein would say the couple have stopped sharing a language-game, where the same word ("fair," "ours") now means incompatible things.

**Where the law does its damage.** The adversarial process is a *measurement apparatus* pointed the wrong way. A good measurement finds the overlap between two frames. Advocacy does the opposite. It drives each client *deeper* into their own frame ("you're right, the law agrees, push"). Two measurements pulling apart. The recoverable shared state decoheres. By month eighteen it is destroyed. That is why the late settlement is *worse* than the early one, not merely more expensive.

In Buber's terms, the process converts the spouse from a *Thou* (a person) into an *It* (an asset-holder, an obstacle). Resentment is the residue of an I–Thou collapsed into I–It. The law industrialises that collapse, then sells it back by the hour to people meeting a lawyer for the second time in their lives, who mistake "the law is on your side" for physics.

## The reframe: not an arbiter of truth

Treating settlement as a maths problem is looking for the *correct answer*. There is no correct answer. That is the *Rashomon* point. Any product that tries to find whose reality is true fails the way the lawyers fail, just cheaper.

The right job is **Gadamer's *fusion of horizons* (*Horizontverschmelzung*)**: understanding between two frames happens not by one winning, nor by averaging, but by building a *third* horizon both can stand in without feeling their reality was erased.

A settlement, properly understood, is not a number. **It is the smallest shared reality both people can live inside.** A *durable* settlement, one that doesn't come back, doesn't get re-litigated, is exactly a state that does not decohere: it holds because both horizons are represented in it, not because one was defeated.

---

## The seven guidelines

Each is a design rule, not a sentiment. The architecture must obey them.

1. **Two frames, never merged into one "truth."** Capture each party's reality as a first-class, valid account. Don't reconcile by fact-checking or averaging. Segregated intake is *epistemics*, not just privacy. Each frame stays intact.

2. **The AI is a translator, not a judge.** Its core operation is making each frame *legible to the other*: surfacing the need under the position in language the other horizon can hear.

3. **Refuse the "who's right" question, structurally.** The system never adjudicates truth. The band is not "the correct answer"; it is the zone where the two horizons *overlap*.

4. **Optimise for the state that doesn't decohere.** Score settlements for "will both still stand in this in two years," not "is this maximally favourable to one side." Durability over victory.

5. **Preserve the *Thou*.** Never reduce the other party to an asset-holder or an opponent. (This is why there is no partisan weak-point or "attack prep" feature: the moment you build it, you are a cheaper lawyer, not a different thing.)

6. **Speed is epistemic protection, not just cost.** The longer it runs adversarially, the more the frames harden past the point of fusion. Settle before decoherence. This is *why* the fee-pump model cannot build this product.

7. **Show the user there are two valid realities.** Part of the repair is the user *seeing*, gently, without being told they're wrong, that their account is not the only coherent one. Reflect the other's frame back as valid-from-their-side. No adversarial lawyer will ever do this for them.

---

*Note the convergence: the philosophy, the legal pass ([`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md)), and the product ethics arrive at the same guidelines: no coercion, no attack-prep, preserve both frames, optimise for durability. When metaphysics, law, and design all point at one architecture, it's probably real.*
