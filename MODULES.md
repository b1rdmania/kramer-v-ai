# The three modules

These fall directly out of [`PHILOSOPHY.md`](PHILOSOPHY.md). Each module exists to serve specific guidelines; if a feature serves none of them, it doesn't belong.

```
  Frame Capture  ──▶  Horizon Fusion  ──▶  Durable Settlement
  (two realities,     (find the overlap,    (make the shared reality
   intact)             not the winner)        real, and make it hold)
```

---

## Module 1 — Frame Capture

*Serves guidelines 1 (two frames), 7 (show two realities), and the safeguarding gate.*

Capture each party's reality, emotional and financial, as a first-class, valid, **segregated** account. Never merged, never fact-checked into a single "truth."

- **Emotional discovery.** Empathetic intake (Gottman / EFT frameworks). Surfaces the need under the stated position. Listens; does not advise or correct.
- **Financial disclosure.** Form E-style intake, simplified for negotiation.
- **Safeguarding is the first gate.** Before anything proceeds: domestic-abuse / coercion screening (FPR PD3A / MIAM norms). On a signal → escalate, do not negotiate. Quick-exit UX. See [`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md).

The AI's job here is **to listen and to validate a frame**, not to evaluate it.

---

## Module 2 — Horizon Fusion

*Serves guidelines 2 (translator), 3 (refuse "who's right"), 4 (durability).*

Find the zone where the two frames **overlap**, and translate each frame into language the other can hear. The output is a **settlement band**: the range a court would likely sanction under s.25 MCA 1973, presented as the overlap region, never as "the answer."

- Reasoned over the facts and the two emotional reads. The emotional weighting shifts the overlap: a driver of shame-around-fatherhood means time-with-children is *over-valued* by that party, so the durable zone moves.
- **LLM-reasoned, not a game-theory solver.** "Nash" is a framing, not an implementation.
- Scored for **durability** ("will both still stand in this in two years"), not for advantage to either side.

The AI's job here is **translation and overlap-finding**, not judgment.

---

## Module 3 — Durable Settlement

*Serves guidelines 4 (durability), 5 (preserve the Thou), 6 (speed/no decoherence).*

Render the shared reality concrete, and build it to hold.

- **Open offer** drafted from the band (the costs-bearing instrument in financial remedy, *not* Calderbank; FPR 28.3). Standard family-law shape.
- **Human sign-off gates.** Fixed-fee, limited-scope review (see the unbundled model in [`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md)). The signer is configurable — a solicitor each side, one solicitor, a neutral mediator, none, or advice-only. No partisan attack-prep.
- **Audit chain.** Every gate emits an artefact (who / when / on what evidence / override notes). This is the **UK GDPR Art 22 mechanism** that keeps the settlement out of "solely automated" territory, and the SRA-durability spine. It is the defensible asset.

The settlement binds only via a court-approved consent order. The court is the fairness backstop, by design.
