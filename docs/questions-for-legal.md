# Questions for a legal partner

*The product is built loop-agnostic: the human-in-the-loop configuration is a dial, not a baked-in choice (see [`../MODULES.md`](../MODULES.md), Module 3). These are the questions a family solicitor — a hackathon contact, or a prospective co-founder — can actually answer, and which let us turn the dial with confidence rather than guesswork. Good test of a co-founder, too: watch how they reason about these.*

## 1. The supervision dial — which settings are lawful and insurable?

The same engine can run at different supervision levels. Which of these are clean in E&W, and which is the best *first* setting?

- **Two solicitors**, one each side, each signs their client's gate.
- **One solicitor**, one side advised; the other a litigant-in-person.
- **Neutral mediator** — serves both, gives information not advice.
- **No solicitor** — a regulated entity is itself the authority (the North Star).
- **Advisory-only** — information, not advice; user takes it where they like.

## 2. What can the output call itself at each setting?

Where is the line between *information*, *advice*, and a *signed offer*? Given that general advice is unreserved (LSA 2007), what wording / framing keeps each configuration on the right side of the SRA and consumer-protection rules?

## 3. What is a *real* sign-off (not a rubber stamp)?

Where a solicitor signs a gate: what is the minimum substantive review needed to carry liability on the resulting consent order? What must a fixed-fee, limited-scope (unbundled) retainer actually say to define scope and avoid being treated as a full retainer?

## 4. Safeguarding — what's the professional standard?

What domestic-abuse / coercive-control screening would a family solicitor expect before negotiation? Where must a human take over from the AI, and what does responsible escalation look like? (Working assumption: screen first, escalate not negotiate — confirm it's enough.)

## 5. Durability — what makes a settlement survive?

What should the product capture to make a consent order most likely to survive challenge for non-disclosure, duress, or undue influence (*Sharland*, *Gohil*)? (Disclosure confirmations, pressure checks, audit trail — are these the right ones?)

## 6. The no-lawyer endgame

Realistically, what authorisation route (SRA / ABS) and what ownership structure works for a non-solicitor-owned entity? Would a solicitor personally take principal liability, and on what terms?

## 7. PI insurance

Who underwrites a product like this, and what do they need to see before they'll quote at a workable price?
