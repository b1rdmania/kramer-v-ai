# Kramer v AI: Emotive Narrative

*Phase 0 of the brand-skill. The soul, written before any visual work. Every later design choice is testable against this.*

## Research foundation (Phase 0, Step 2)

From: attrition, resentment, fear, being unseen (fear + anger + sadness)

## The Human Moment

It's the kitchen table where they used to plan holidays. Now there's a solicitor's letter on it, and reading it she feels something close in her chest: the man she has just described to her lawyer and the man across the hall have stopped being the same person. He feels it too. Each has begun, quietly, to build a case against someone they once chose. Neither set out to. The letter just made it the only language left.

## The Deeper Truth

There is no neutral account of a marriage ending. Each of them is telling the truth — their own truth, whole and coherent, and irreconcilable with the other's. The law's answer to two truths is to pick a side and press, and to bill by the hour while it presses. The longer it runs, the further the two realities drift, until the settlement that finally arrives at month eighteen is worse, and dearer, than the one that was within reach in week two. The process profits from the distance it manufactures.

## The Transformation

Imagine the opposite. Each person heard in full, on their own terms, by something that isn't keeping score. The thing neither of them would say out loud, surfaced gently and kept private. Then the small patch of shared ground located — the only place a settlement can actually stand — and an offer built to live there. Not a victory. A settlement that holds, because both of them are inside it. The fighting stops earlier. The damage stops compounding. They keep some dignity, and the children keep two parents who can still stand in a room together.

## The Ethos

We don't look for who is right; there is no such answer to find. We refuse to turn either person into an opponent or an asset to be won. We build the smallest reality both can live in, and we judge ourselves on whether it lasts. Safety comes before all of it: if there is fear in the room, the machine stops and a human steps in. And no consequential moment passes without a person putting their name to it. Here, speed is mercy — the sooner two people stop fighting, the less of each other they destroy.

## The Personality

Grave, and warm with it. This is grief work and it knows that; it never reaches for cheerfulness or the bright optimism of a launch. It has the calm of a good, quiet room and the steadiness of someone who has sat with people on their worst day and not looked away. Precise where precision earns trust, gentle everywhere else. It does not show off. It would rather be plain than clever. Less an app, more a careful presence that carries some of the weight so the two of them don't have to carry all of it alone.

## The North Star

When a choice is hard, one question settles it: does this help these two people stand on shared ground, or does it drive them apart? Anything that widens the gap — a clever nudge, a sharper edge, a number dressed up as certainty — is wrong here, however well it tests. Anything that lets them be heard, stay safe, and end this with dignity is the way. We are not trying to win the divorce. We are trying to end the war.
