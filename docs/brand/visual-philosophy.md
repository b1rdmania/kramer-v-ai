# Solemn Warmth

*Phase 1 of the brand-skill — the emotional and aesthetic **territory**, not a spec. This is a feeling and a metaphor to design against, so that whatever gets made can be tested against it. It does not decide type, colour, layout, logo, or motion. Those are for Variant, by eye.*

## The feeling

**Warm gravity.** The calm of a serious, kind room — somewhere grief can be set down and held with dignity, not hurried along or cheered up. Unhurried. Restrained, because restraint reads as care here. Steady, like someone who has sat with people on their worst day and not looked away.

Nothing slick, nothing clinical, nothing that performs cleverness. The opposite of a bright launch and the opposite of a cold dashboard. If a choice draws attention to itself, it's wrong; the work is to help two people put something heavy down, not to impress them.

## The metaphor to design against

**The overlap — the seam.** Two fields meeting into a single patch of shared ground. Convergence, not opposition. The name holds the same tension: *Kramer v AI* reads as *versus*, but everything the brand does says *together*. Whatever the design becomes, it should somehow carry "two becoming one place they can both stand."

## Feels like / never feels like

**Feels like:** a quiet room after the argument ends · dusk light · weight, held carefully · someone serious and kind who has been expecting you · being heard.

**Never feels like:** a startup launch · a clinical dashboard or a confidence score · cold legal-tech · a clever data-viz · anything that sells or shows off.

## Left to Variant

Type, colour, layout, logo, motion — all of it. Decide them against the feeling and the metaphor above, by eye, diverge / kill / mutate. Don't reach for the safe defaults (the serif-plus-mono pairing, the considered-startup palette); that reflex is exactly what makes everything look the same. The brand's job here is to give Variant a soul to serve, not a system to follow.

*Solemn Warmth: gravity you can trust with the worst week of your life.*
